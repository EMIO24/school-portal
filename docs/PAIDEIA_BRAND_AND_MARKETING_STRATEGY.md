# Paideia Brand and Marketing Strategy

## Brand Decision

### Name

# Paideia

### Greek origin

**παιδεία** (*paideia*)

Paideia means education as the cultivation of the whole person: knowledge,
character, discipline, judgment and human development. This meaning fits a
platform that connects school administration, teaching, assessment, parents and
student growth.

### Recommended pronunciation

**pie-DAY-ah**

### Primary tagline

> **Cultivating minds. Building futures.**

### Product description

> Paideia is a complete digital school platform for managing school operations,
teaching, assessment, payments and parent communication in one connected place.

## Logo System

### Logo idea

The Paideia logo is an elegant **P monogram built from an open book and a rising
path**.

- The left stroke of the `P` represents the first page of an open book.
- The bowl of the `P` represents a protected learning community.
- A fine upward path through the mark represents progress and student growth.
- A restrained laurel curve may be used as a secondary detail to represent
  excellence, but it should never overpower the monogram.
- The mark must work without a graduation cap, cartoon student or literal school
  building.

### Simple visual direction

```text
       __________
      /          \
     /   PAIDEIA  \
    |      P      |
    |   book +    |
    | rising path |
     \___________/
```

The final production artwork should be geometric, balanced and created as a
vector mark. The sketch above describes the relationship between the elements,
not the final artwork.

### Logo versions

| Version | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Primary lockup | Paideia monogram beside the wordmark |
| Compact lockup | Monogram above the wordmark |
| App mark | Monogram only inside a square or soft-rectangle frame |
| One-color mark | Black or white version for forms, receipts and stamps |
| Small-size mark | Simplified `P` book shape without fine laurel detail |

### Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the inner opening of the
`P`. Do not place text, borders or other icons inside this area.

### Do not

- Stretch or compress the logo.
- Add gradients, shadows or bevel effects.
- Use more than two brand colors in the mark.
- Place the logo over busy photography.
- Replace the wordmark with an unapproved font.
- Use the laurel as the main symbol.

## Visual Identity

### Color palette

| Name | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Navy | `#102A43` | Primary brand, navigation, headings |
| Ivory | `#F7F3EA` | Warm document and marketing background |
| Antique Gold | `#B08D57` | Premium accents, achievements, highlights |
| Deep Teal | `#1F5A5A` | Teaching, learning and positive actions |
| Charcoal | `#263238` | Body text |
| Mist | `#E8EEF2` | Borders, panels and quiet backgrounds |
| Success Green | `#287A57` | Confirmed payments and completed work |
| Alert Red | `#B84242` | Errors and urgent actions |

### Color proportions

```text
Midnight Navy  45%  structure and trust
Ivory          25%  space and warmth
Mist           15%  surfaces and separation
Deep Teal      10%  actions and learning states
Antique Gold    5%  emphasis and premium moments
```

Gold should remain an accent. Large gold backgrounds can make the product feel
like a financial service rather than an education platform.

### Typography

| Use | Font direction |
| --- | --- |
| Wordmark and major brand headings | Cormorant Garamond or Libre Baskerville |
| Product interface | Manrope or Source Sans 3 |
| Tables and PDF documents | Source Sans 3 or a reliable system sans-serif |
| Numbers and financial totals | Manrope with tabular numerals |

The wordmark should feel classical and editorial. The application itself should
remain highly readable, calm and efficient.

- Warm without being childish
- Premium without being expensive or distant
- Practical without sounding like accounting software
- Confident without making unrealistic promises

### Preferred language

- learner progress

- school leadership

Avoid:

## Core Marketing Position

### One-sentence position

> Paideia gives schools one calm, connected place to run the school day, support teachers and keep families informed.

### Main customer problem

Many schools operate across paper records, spreadsheets, WhatsApp messages,
separate fee books and disconnected exam tools. Information is duplicated,
missed or difficult for school leaders to verify.

### Paideia promise

> From lesson planning to payment records, Paideia keeps the important work of a
school connected, visible and accountable.

### Differentiators

1. One workflow from lesson plan to assessment and result.
2. Per-student school billing that is clear before payment.
3. Tenant-safe records for schools, families and platform owners.
4. Practical tools designed for real school staff, not only technology teams.
5. A complete digital path for teaching, assignments, CA and online exams.

## Target Customers

### Primary segment

Private primary and secondary schools with approximately 100 to 500 students.

Characteristics:

- The owner or principal still makes operational decisions.
- Staff use smartphones daily but may not have dedicated IT support.
- The school relies on paper, spreadsheets or messaging groups.
- Parents expect faster access to results, fees and announcements.
- End-of-term reporting creates significant administrative pressure.

### Secondary segment

School groups and larger institutions that need Enterprise organization reporting,
central oversight and integrations.

### First geographic focus

Begin with one reachable local market and build references there before expanding.
Local trust, onboarding and support will be more valuable than broad advertising
at the start.

## Pricing Message

The subscription is charged per active student per term:

```text
Basic:   NGN 1,200 per active student per term
Premium: NGN 2,000 per active student per term
Enterprise: proposed NGN 3,000 per active student per term
```

Schools with 100 or more active students receive the configured 10% discount.
The product must show the school's estimated total before checkout.

Marketing must never describe the plans as flat school fees.

## Launch Strategy

### Phase 1: Proof and preparation

Goal: make the product easy to understand and safe to demonstrate.

Actions:

- Finalize the Paideia logo and brand assets.
- Create a short demo school with realistic but synthetic data.
- Prepare a 10 to 15 minute guided product demonstration.
- Record short screen videos for lesson plans, assignments, results and payments.
- Prepare a one-page PDF explaining the per-student pricing.
- Create a simple landing page with a direct demo request action.

Success signal:

- A school owner understands the product and pricing after one demonstration.

### Phase 2: Pilot schools

Recruit 5 to 10 schools for one controlled term.

Offer:

- Guided setup
- Staff onboarding session
- Data import assistance
- Weekly support check-in
- Pilot pricing or setup incentive

Require:

- A named school decision-maker
- A small staff champion group
- Permission to collect feedback
- A scheduled onboarding date
- A written definition of pilot success

Measure:

- Time to onboard staff
- Number of active weekly users
- Assignment submission rate
- Result preparation time
- Parent engagement
- Support requests per school
- Renewal intent

### Phase 3: Local growth

Use proof from the pilot schools:

- Short owner testimonials
- Before-and-after workflow stories
- Screenshots with permission
- End-of-term time saved
- Parent communication improvements
- Teacher adoption stories

Channels:

- Direct school visits
- WhatsApp school-owner groups
- Private-school associations
- Teacher training events
- Education consultants
- Local ICT and school-equipment partners
- Referral introductions from existing schools

### Phase 4: Repeatable sales

Create a consistent sales funnel:

```text
Awareness
    |
    v
Short practical school-management content
    |
    v
WhatsApp or website demo request
    |
    v
15-minute product demonstration
    |
    v
School workflow and student-count assessment
    |
    v
Guided pilot or paid onboarding
    |
    v
Termly subscription renewal
    |
    v
Referral and expansion
```

## Content Strategy

Publish content that solves visible school problems.

### Weekly content themes

| Day | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Teacher workflow tip |
| Tuesday | School-owner operations problem |
| Wednesday | Product demonstration |
| Thursday | Parent communication or student progress topic |
| Friday | Customer story, checklist or end-of-week insight |

### Example topics

- How to prepare result sheets without combining multiple spreadsheets
- How teachers can collect assignments online
- How to keep continuous assessment records organized
- How parents can follow linked children without calling the office
- How school owners can see outstanding fees clearly
- How a lesson plan can connect to notes, assignments and exams
- What schools should prepare before moving from paper records

## Sales Demonstration Script

1. Start with the school's current process and pain points.
2. Show the school dashboard and student count.
3. Add or import a student.
4. Create a lesson plan.
5. Open teaching notes and projection mode.
6. Create an assignment and submit a student response.
7. Create a CA activity or online exam.
8. Mark and publish feedback.
9. Show the parent and student view.
10. Show the result PDF and fee receipt.
11. Display the exact term total based on the school's active student count.
12. Explain onboarding, support and the next step.

Do not begin with a long feature list. Show one connected school day.

## Brand Experience Inside The Product

Apply Paideia consistently to:

- Login screens
- School dashboard
- Navigation shell
- Payment page
- PDF receipts
- Result slips
- Broadsheets
- User guides
- Email and SMS templates
- Empty states and error messages

The product should feel like one system across screen and paper. PDF documents
should use the same navy, ivory, teal and gold hierarchy as the application.

## Trust and Compliance Messaging

Marketing claims must remain accurate:

- State that payment verification happens on the server.
- Explain that plans are billed per active student per term.
- Do not promise unlimited SMS or email unless commercially supported.
- Do not describe planned Enterprise capabilities as available.
- Do not use real student data in public demonstrations.
- Explain that schools remain responsible for approving and publishing results.
- Publish support contact and escalation expectations.

## Key Metrics

Track monthly:

- Demo requests
- Demo-to-pilot conversion
- Pilot-to-paid conversion
- Active schools
- Active students
- Teacher weekly retention
- Assignment submission rate
- Parent weekly engagement
- Payment completion rate
- Support tickets per school
- Renewal rate
- Referral rate

The most important early metric is not website traffic. It is the number of
schools that continue using Paideia after the first term.
