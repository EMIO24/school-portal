# Acceptable Use Policy — draft

**LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.** Proposed practical rules for school-authorized use;
not a final legally reviewed policy. Enforcement, notices and remedies require
approval in the school agreement.

Use Paideia only through accounts and school records you are authorized to access.
Do not:

- Access another person's account or attempt cross-school access.
- Share personal credentials with unauthorized people or circumvent roles,
  tenant boundaries, parent links or account restrictions.
- Upload malware, malicious files or content the school is not authorized to use.
- Manipulate CBT/examination systems, impersonate candidates or obtain protected
  assessment information without authorization.
- Submit fraudulent payments or attempt to falsify payment verification/receipts.
- Scrape protected student or staff information, or misuse educational data.
- Attack availability, overload services or bypass security controls.
- Use school/student information unlawfully or disclose protected records without
  appropriate authorization.

Schools should review account access, keep data accurate and report suspected
misuse through their agreed support channel. Users should contact their school
administrator for account issues. The existing public enquiry route is `/demo`,
linked from `/contact`; it is not a secure incident-file upload channel. Never
submit passwords, payment credentials or detailed student records there.

Existing suspension/account controls can restrict access without erasing school
history. Contractual grounds, notice, response procedures and any legal reporting
obligations remain **BUSINESS / LEGAL DECISION REQUIRED**. This draft establishes
no automatic penalty or deletion rule.
