# Legal Review Checklist

**LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED.** All items are unresolved until an appropriate reviewer
records a decision, approving person and document version. These drafts are not
legal advice, certification or evidence of statutory compliance.

- [ ] Confirm [PAIDEIA LEGAL ENTITY], school contracting identity, authority and
  verified business registration details; publish only approved information.
- [ ] Confirm official contact channels and the privacy/security request process.
- [ ] Determine governing law/jurisdiction and dispute-resolution provisions.
- [ ] Establish formal data protection roles and any processing agreement or
  instructions; do not infer controller/processor status from software behavior.
- [ ] Review lawful handling of children's/student data, school authority and
  parental responsibility/consent requirements where applicable.
- [ ] Review sensitive optional demographics, including religion: appropriate
  collection, necessity, restricted access and notice requirements.
- [ ] Approve category-specific retention periods and deletion/anonymization rights
  and procedures, including backups, exported copies and provider storage.
- [ ] Determine security incident/data breach responsibilities and notifications.
- [ ] Review refund rights, payment disputes, consumer protection and billing
  disclosures; decide business policy without overriding applicable rights.
- [ ] Review liability limitations, warranties and indemnity obligations.
- [ ] Finalize confidentiality, permitted disclosures and intellectual-property
  provisions for platform materials and school-owned content/data.
- [ ] Review actual third-party services, contracts, data locations/transfers and
  any subprocessors; make no unsupported provider compliance claims.
- [ ] Finalize suspension grounds, notices/remediation, termination consequences,
  outstanding payments, school export arrangements and deletion implications.
- [ ] Approve service/term-change procedures and how final documents are accepted.
- [ ] Confirm support commitments and recovery/availability language against
  verified operational capability; do not invent an SLA.
- [ ] Approve and version the public Terms/Privacy pages, school agreement, AUP,
  payment/refund draft and retention framework before treating them as final.
